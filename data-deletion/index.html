How to request

Send us an email

To begin, send an email to our support team with the subject line:

Subject“Data Deletion Request”

Include the following information so we can locate and verify your account:

  • ✓Full name
  • ✓Email address associated with the account
  • ✓Phone number associated with the account, if applicable
  • ✓ConnectStream service or business account
  • ✓Description of the data you want deleted

We may request additional information to verify your identity before processing the request.
