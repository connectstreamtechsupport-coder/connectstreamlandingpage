DATA RIGHTS & COMPLIANCE CENTER

Data Deletion Instructions

In compliance with QFC Data Protection Regulations, GDPR Article 17 (Right to Erasure), and Meta Platform Data Deletion requirements, you can request full erasure of your account, telemetry, and customer communication logs.

01

How to Submit a Deletion Request

Send an email from your registered account administrative address to our dedicated data privacy desk at privacy@connectstream.io with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".

Include the following verification details:
  • Full Account Name & Registered Entity Name
  • Registered Admin Email Address
  • Connectstream Workspace ID / Subdomain
  • Connected WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) Phone Number(s)
  • Scope of deletion: (Full Workspace Deletion / Selective Contact Log Purge)
02

What Happens After Submission

STEP 1 • 24 HOURS

Identity & Ownership Verification

We authenticate the request against registered workspace admin credentials to prevent unauthorized account tampering.

STEP 2 • 48 HOURS

Data Inventory Review

We identify all active database records, webhooks, contact lists, media assets, and chat history associated with the account.

STEP 3 • 72 HOURS

Cryptographic Erasure

All customer identifiers, webhook payloads, and communication logs are permanently expunged from primary storage and replicated backups.

STEP 4 • COMPLETION

Formal Certificate of Erasure

An official confirmation of data erasure is emailed to your designated contact for audit and regulatory compliance archives.

Notice Regarding Upstream WhatsApp End-User Communications

When Connectstream services are used by enterprise businesses to communicate with end customers, message copies stored on user devices are controlled locally by those individuals and by Meta Platforms, Inc. according to WhatsApp Terms of Service. Enterprise account erasure from Connectstream removes all cloud broker cache, telemetry, and middle-layer records permanently.

Need assistance with your data request? Our compliance officers in Doha respond within 1 business day.
